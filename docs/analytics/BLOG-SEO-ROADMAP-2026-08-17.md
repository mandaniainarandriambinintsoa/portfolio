## Data snapshot

- GSC current period: 150 clicks, 6,274 impressions, 2.39% CTR, average position 11.99.
- Previous period: 128 clicks, 4,551 impressions, 2.81% CTR, average position 9.85.
- PostHog: 78 observed page paths and 117 identified opportunity pages.
- SerpApi research: 12 Google Trends requests and 8 Madagascar SERP requests.

The site is gaining visibility, but clicks are growing more slowly than impressions. The priority is therefore to improve topical coverage and CTR around queries already associated with Manda, rather than creating unrelated content.

## Publishing cadence

- Week 1: publish one article from Priority A and improve the related service page title/description from its GSC queries.
- Week 2: publish one emerging topic from Priority B.
- Week 3: update an existing article that already has impressions but low CTR.
- Week 4: publish one local commercial article and add one external distribution asset (n8n Community, GitHub, LinkedIn or a relevant forum).

Each article should receive a GSC review after 28 days and again after 90 days. Track impressions, CTR, position, engaged sessions, 75% scroll completion and contact CTA clicks. Do not judge a page only by pageviews.

## Guardrails

- Do not create another exact-match `agent IA Facebook` sales page.
- Do not use `GenAI` alone as the primary French keyword; use it as a synonym inside `IA generative` content.
- Keep financial, legal and platform policy claims qualified and sourced.
- Add a real project, screenshot or workflow whenever an article makes a capability claim.
- Update existing pages before creating near-duplicate articles.
